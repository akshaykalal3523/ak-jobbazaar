📋

Equal Opportunity Hiring

AK Jobbazaar is an equal-opportunity employer. All applicants are evaluated solely on merit, skills, and qualifications — regardless of gender, caste, religion, age, or disability.

🔍

Background Verification

All selected candidates undergo mandatory background and reference checks including academic credentials, previous employment verification, and criminal record clearance before joining.

📅

Timeline Commitment

We commit to completing end-to-end hiring within 21 working days — from job posting to offer letter issuance — to respect candidate time and business urgency.

🤝

Internal Mobility First

Open positions are first shared internally for 5 days. Internal applicants meeting 80% of requirements are given priority before external hiring begins.

📊

Structured Interviews

All interviews are competency-based with standardised rubrics. Panel interviews require minimum two evaluators. Scores are documented on the ATS platform.

🔒

Confidentiality & GDPR

All candidate data is stored securely and used only for recruitment purposes. Data is deleted within 12 months of rejection unless candidate opts into the talent pool.
